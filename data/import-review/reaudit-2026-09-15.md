## Invoice-number rule

The client confirmed that `BIS` in a filename means the entered invoice number ends in `/BIS`. Use the printed series (`AI` or `AL`) and append `/BIS` after it. The five BIS files are distinct entries under this rule, although their PDFs print the base number without the suffix:

| --- | --- | --- |
| INV 23 BIS WWL MOROCCO | 023/2026/AI/BIS | 23/2026/AI |
| INV 24 BIS CHINA CONSTRUCTION OCP OCT-NOV | 024/2026/AI/BIS | 24/2026/AI |
| INV 29 BIS MINGDIAN TANGER Déc | 029/2026/AI/BIS | 029/2026/AI |
| INV 30 BIS MINGDIAN TANGER JAN | 030/2026/AI/BIS | 030/2026/AI |
| INV 43 BIS MINGDIAN TANGER AVRIL | 043/2026/AI/BIS | 043/2026/AI |

The leading zero on the entered number is a normalized review convention; the original printed form is retained in the source PDF.

`INV 003 COVEC` is printed `003/2026/AL`, while `INV 003 GOTION` is printed `003/2026/AI`: these are different series, so the previous numeric-prefix duplicate flag was false. The user retained `INV 47 AGL Mai.pdf` for `047/2026/AI`; the other version is no longer in the folder. The retained file's numeric TTC is 651,000 DH, but its amount in words still says 639,000 DH.
